1. Add your info

It prints on the letter so the business knows who to call. Leave any box blank to get a line you can write on by hand.

2. Print

On a phone, Print opens your phone's print menu, where you can print or save as a PDF to print somewhere else.

Tips for the visit

  • Go when it's slow, and ask for the owner or manager. If they're out, leave the letter and a sign-up card.
  • Keep it short and friendly. The letter does the explaining.
  • If they say yes to being a drop-off spot, bring the window sign and a box with labels, and set a pickup day.
  • Back in the hub, log the business on the Printables tab so the crew knows who's helping and pickups get scheduled.
  • A “no” is fine! Thank them, and leave a letter in case they change their mind.
  • Ask if you can leave a stack of GoFundMe QR cards by the register. Lots of people would rather give a few dollars than shop.

Letter to the business

The Kilted Santa Project

Christmas gifts for local families, delivered by Santas in kilts · Jackson County, Michigan

Dear friends at ,

Every year, right before Christmas, a crew of Santas in kilts drives across Jackson County bringing gifts to local families who could use a little extra joy. Families sign up, neighbors adopt them, and our volunteers (we call ourselves the ELVES: Enthusiastic Loving Volunteers Empowering Santa) shop, wrap and load the sleigh. It's all volunteer-run, and every gift stays right here in our community.

We'd love to have you on the team. Here are a few ways your business can help. Pick one, or a few:

🎁 Be a gift drop-off spotWe bring a decorated box and a sign for your window, and an ELF picks up gifts on a set schedule.
🧸 Donate new, unwrapped giftsToys, books, games, and warm things like coats, hats, gloves and boots.
🎀 Donate wrapping suppliesPaper, tape, tags, gift bags and tissue. We go through a lot!
💳 Donate gift cardsGreat for teens, and for household needs like groceries and gas.
🎄 Adopt a family as a teamWe'll match your business with a local family and their wish list.
📣 Help spread the wordShare our posts or hang a flyer so families and givers can find us.
1Families sign up, and our volunteers check that each one is local and in real need.
2Neighbors, churches and businesses adopt families and shop their wish lists.
3ELVES wrap and load, and Santas in kilts deliver on Dec 23 and 24.

What happens next: fill out the sign-up card that came with this letter and hand it back to me, or give me a call. I'll take care of the rest.

Rather give online? Scan the GoFundMe code below. Every bit helps fill Santa's sleigh.

Thank you for helping make Christmas brighter for families right here at home.

With gratitude,
, Kilted Santa ELF
