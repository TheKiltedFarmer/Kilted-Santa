Welcome to the crew!

Every year right before Christmas, Santas in kilts drive across Jackson County bringing gifts and good cheer to families who could use a little extra joy. Families sign up, neighbors adopt them, Santa swings by as many homes as he can, and you, the ELVES, make the whole thing happen.

The ELVES run the show. Justin steers the project and jumps in where he's needed, but the day-to-day belongs to the crew. Santa's Operations Hub is where we do that together: it shows what needs doing, who's on it, and which families still need help.

💡 The one-sentence version: open the hub, look at Ways to help right now on the Home tab, tap I'll do it on something that fits you, and mark it done when you're finished.

Getting into the hub

  1. Sign up to volunteer on the Kilted Santa page, under "I want to volunteer." Check off everything you're interested in.
  2. Wait for your ELF code. A Lead looks over new volunteers and sends you a code like ELF-7KQ4P2, by email or text.
  3. Open the hub at thepeoplesvoices.org/Kilted-Santa/hub.html. Sign in with the email or phone number you signed up with, plus your code. Leave "Keep me signed in" checked on your own phone.
  4. Put it on your home screen so it opens like an app:
    • iPhone: in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
🔒 Your ELF code is like a key. It opens up details about the families we serve, so please don't share it, post it, or sign in on someone else's phone. Lost it? Ask a Lead for a new one.

FamiliesWho we're helping, and how their gifts are coming along
  • Every family has a number like KS-004. Swing-by requests start with SB-. Tap a family to open it.

Adoption ELVES and Leads also get:

  • Needs review: new families waiting to be checked. Use View proof of address and View family photo (they're never shared outside the adoption team), then tap ✅ Approve: proof checks out to put them on the Adopt page.
  • ⚠️ Flagged means the sign-up uses the same phone or address as a family already signed up, or one we've removed. Look closely before approving.
  • 🙋 I'll handle this family marks you as their point person, so two ELVES don't call the same family.
  • Record an adopter for adoptions that come in by phone or in person, or Release one that fell through so the family goes back on the Adopt page.
  • 🗑 Remove takes a family off the hub and the Adopt page if something doesn't add up. You'll be asked for a reason, and the record is kept in the sheet.

RoutesSanta teams and their stops on Dec 23 and 24
  • Join a Santa team by typing its name at the top and tapping Save. Ask your team in Chat what it's called.
  • Check "I'm comfortable doing random stops too" if you're OK visiting families nobody adopted, and swing-by requests. Otherwise you'll only get adopted families.

🎲 Random stops are families nobody adopted, plus swing-by requests. They go on Santa Justin's route unless a Santa who's comfortable with them takes them. None of them are promised to the family.

How the season flows

November 1: sign-ups open

Families sign up, Adoption ELVES check and approve them, stories go out, and the crew starts visiting businesses for drop-off spots.

November into early December: adoptions

Neighbors adopt families. Shoppers and adopters fill wish lists. Drivers start pickup runs from drop-off spots.

Mid-December: wrapping

Gifts come in, wrapping parties happen, and families move to Wrapped. Adoption ELVES and Santa teams finish the routes.

Just before the big night: loading

Gifts are sorted by route and loaded up. Families are marked Loaded.

Dec 23 and 24: Santa's on the road 🎅

Santa teams deliver, swing-bys happen where the sleigh can fit them, and the feed lights up with deliveries.

After Christmas

Thank the businesses and adopters (only by name if they said OK), and get some rest. You earned it.

Golden rules

  1. Never post or share a family's photo. Not even a sweet one, and not even with permission. Story posts are first names, ages, the story and the link. That's all.
  2. Private stays private. Addresses, phone numbers, last names and proof documents stay in the hub. Use family numbers (KS-004) when you talk about families.
  3. No promises. We can't guarantee every family gets adopted, or when Santa will swing by. If a family asks, be kind and honest: "We're doing everything we can, and we'll be in touch."
  4. Families come first, judgment never. Everyone who signs up has been through something. Be warm, and let the adoption team do the checking.
  5. Claim it, then do it. If you grab a task and life happens, let it go so someone else can pick it up.
  6. When in doubt, ask in Chat. Someone's always happy to help.

Questions

I lost my ELF code, or it stopped working.
Ask a Lead. They can send you a new one from the Crew tab in a few seconds.
Why can't I see a family's address?
That's on purpose. You'll see the address once the family is on your Santa team's route. Adoption ELVES and Leads see them all.
How do I join or switch Santa teams?
On the Routes tab, type the team name next to "Your Santa team" and tap Save.
Something looks out of date.
Tap Refresh at the top of the hub. If the hub itself was just updated, close the tab and open it again.
A family asked me directly when Santa is coming.
Thank them warmly, don't promise a time, and let the Adoption team know in Chat.
A business wants to know if their donation is tax-deductible.
Don't guess. Let them know a Lead will follow up, and post the question in Chat.
I want to help but I'm not sure where.
Open Home, look at Ways to help right now, and pick the first thing that makes you smile.
